hashtag#sustainability tip: don’t use ChatGPT when Google could give you the answer!

A Google search costs up to 1.08kJ. A ChatGPT-4 request in the range of 3.6-36 kJ.

So that's 3-36 times more energy that you would be spending — and often you can skip the hallucination and the source-checking: it’s often already there when you Google it

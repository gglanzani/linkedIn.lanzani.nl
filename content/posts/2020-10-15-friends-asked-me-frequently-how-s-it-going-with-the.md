I reply we have only reasons to celebrate: since the summer we have grown our pool of full-time trainers with Marysia Winkels, James Hayward, Steven van Duin, and before Christmas we'll get a new trainer — can't name her yet sorry! 

In November two new marketing colleagues will join — can't wait to tell you who they are.

We are growing double digits (!!) since 2019 and almost 300% compared to 2018.

We have started partnerships abroad with https://aibridge.ch, are scaling up our certifications process for #DataScience with #Python (https://gdd.li/certifiedDS) and Data Science for #ProductOwners (https://gdd.li/certifiedPO), and training across 16 time zones (!!).

Thank you team!

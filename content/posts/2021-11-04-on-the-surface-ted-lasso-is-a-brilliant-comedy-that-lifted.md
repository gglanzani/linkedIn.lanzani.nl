On the surface, Ted Lasso is a brilliant comedy that lifted our spirits when — back in 2020 — we just heard one bad news after the other.

Below the surface though it's a show about managing a team and how achieving long-term greatness often means sacrificing short-term results.

It's has so many lessons if you're a leader or if you just want to be a better team player.

Highly recommended — I gave it a 9 on IMDB!

If you're into #genai, you might have heard the last amazing story of the week: "Google CEO says more than a quarter of the company's new code is created by AI". (article below)

And you might feel left behind: 25% productivity increase* across all your software developers, analysts, etc. WOW!

Where's the catch?

Well, in the famous internet forum Hacker News, a Google employee gives us a bit more details (link: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=42002212). The money quote:

"I work for Google, and I just got done with my work day. I was just writing I guess what you'd call 'AI generated code.'
But the code completion engine is basically just good at finishing the lines I'm writing.
[…]
So basically, it's a helpful productivity tool but it's not doing any engineering at all."

https://www.businessinsider.com/google-earnings-q3-2024-new-code-created-by-ai-2024-10?international=true&amp;r=US&amp;IR=T

*Not really 25%, but you get what I mean.

The town hall Almere in the Netherlands decided, just before the pandemic, to halve how often paper bins are emptied from residential neighborhoods.

The pandemic hit, we all changed how we shop, and after more than two years of data, they still haven’t reintroduced the old schedule or increased the capacity of public recycle points — all while increasing tax income through massive population growth.

Results?

Every Monday, this.

If you are in a position to take decisions, be more data-driven — and try to actually care for the environment!

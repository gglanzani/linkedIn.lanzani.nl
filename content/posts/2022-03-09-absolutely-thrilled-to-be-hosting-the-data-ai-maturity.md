Absolutely thrilled to be hosting the Data & AI Maturity Journey track at the Applied Machine Learning Days conference at the end of the month!

We put up a program together with

- Steven Nooijen — Data & AI Strategy lead at GoDataDriven | Part of Xebia — to talk about the characteristics of Data & AI Organizations
- Andreu Mora — Head of Data Science and Machine Learning at Adyen — to talk about Scaling up Data in a Backend-Heavy Organization
- Dorthe Malmqvist — Portfolio and Project Leader at International Flavors & Fragrances — to be interviewed by yours truly about the role of Learning & Development in the AI Maturity Journey
- Sacha Roggeveen and Melissa Perotti — Head of Data & Analytics and Lead Customer Service Datalab at bol.com — to talk about Data Democratization: from Data Strategy to the Golden Path
- Bianca Scheffler and Deniz Gunaydin — Head Data Culture & Innovation and Senior Data Scientist at Swiss Re f— to talk about Success Criteria for Impactful Data & Analytics Strategy
- Afke Schouten — founder of AI Bridge — hosting a panel discussing what lies ahead: trends and challenges during the AI Maturity journey.

The conference is from the 28th to the 30th of March, in beautiful Lausanne. In 2020 more than 2.000 people were there.

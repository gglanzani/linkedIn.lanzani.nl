Over the past few months, our team worked with StarCompliance to add an AI-powered explanation layer inside the STAR Platform — making complex compliance decisions immediately understandable, without compromising on accuracy or auditability.

The impact:

• Up to 70% less manual review effort
• ~95% accuracy on AI-generated explanations
• Faster decisions, less training overhead

The ingredient that made this work wasn't AI — it was everything around it: domain-specific datasets, LLM-as-a-judge validation, and a relentless focus on outputs that hold up under scrutiny.

Enormous credit to the Xebia team who delivered this and especially Timo Uelen and Russell Poynter and to our partners at StarCompliance for trusting us with a problem this consequential.

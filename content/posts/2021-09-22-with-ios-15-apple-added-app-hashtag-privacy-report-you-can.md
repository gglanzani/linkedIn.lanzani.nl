With iOS 15 Apple added App #Privacy Report.

You can record app activity for 7 days, and then see a report.

I think of myself as a very privacy conscious individual when it comes to installing apps on my phone and giving them permission.

So I turned it on today, and I'm going to use https://github.com/johnspurlock/app-privacy-report-viewer to report back in a week.

It's going to be interesting!

I couldn't be happier that we just launched Sodabricks!

We built Sodabricks to streamline data quality checks in Databricks with the following features in mind:

- Centralized & Consistent: Data quality checks using simple YAML configurations versioned in git!
- Automated Monitoring: Track data quality with automated checks and dashboards, ensuring issues are caught early and trust in your data is maintained.
- Scalable & Collaborative: We designed Sodabricks for teams! Non-technical users can easily define data quality rules, fostering collaboration across engineers and analysts.
- Avoid Bloat: Work natively within Databricks without needing external control planes.

With SodaBricks, you can embed data quality into your workflow — effortlessly and effectively. 🚀

Read the announcement in this post by Marta Radziszewska, with contributions by Virginia López-Gil Pérez, Aletta Tordai, Rodaina Mohamed!
